Meet Our Founder

SENSEI P.M.G.

With more than 30 years in martial arts, Sensei P. M. Gnanasekar holds a 7th Dan Karate Black Belt and 3rd Dan WSKF Black Belt, with 25 Gold, 6 Silver and 7 Bronze medals and two "Best Fighter" honours to his name. He founded Sentou-Ken in 2001 with a vision of building not only skilled practitioners, but disciplined, confident, and responsible individuals.

Our Philosophy

More Than Martial Arts.
A Way of Life.

At Sentou-Ken, training goes beyond kicks, blocks, locks and throws, and punches. Every class builds physical fitness alongside discipline, confidence, and respect — values students carry with them far outside the dojo.

Fitness

Structured training that builds strength, flexibility and stamina at every age.

Discipline

A traditional dojo structure that teaches focus, courage, patience and consistency.

Confidence & Respect

Practical self-defence skills built on a foundation of respect — for instructors, peers, and self.
